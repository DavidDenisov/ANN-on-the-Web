# Paper figures

From the repository root, run `python3 paper/figures/generate.py` with matplotlib and reportlab installed. The generator combines the retained timing and payload summaries with the scored top-1 results. It regenerates the architecture, agreement plots, table rows and derived LaTeX values.

Production uses the pre-enlargement presentation from commit `0e26a88`. The enlarged HTML/CSS snapshot in `client-preview/` is hosted at the separate [paper preview](https://paper-figures.wiki-ann-demo-20261022.pages.dev/client_wiki/). Use that preview for future paper captures. To refresh it, overlay `client-preview/` onto `client_wiki/` in a separate prepared frontend root and deploy branch `paper-figures`; keep these presentation files out of the production (`main`) deployment. The screenshots below use the enlarged paper presentation.

The paper uses the assets below. Both screenshots are framed around their controls and visible results before inclusion; LaTeX uses the complete 4000-pixel-wide PNGs. Separate 2000-pixel-wide PNG copies are retained as alternatives. Both sizes are rendered directly from the interface and recorded results.

- `screenshots/search-desktop-4000w.png`: browser capture (4000×2952 pixels), with a separate `screenshots/search-desktop-2000w.png` copy (2000×1476 pixels), rendered on November 4, 2026 from the existing captured question, visible passage cards and timings. High-density rendering preserves the original 1425-pixel logical layout and the paper's framing, omitting the masthead, bottom and right edges. Search options are expanded to show the probe control. The capture shortens the query field and hides example buttons and helper text to fit the panel. Body text, secondary controls, corpus/result summaries, helper text and passage-distance labels use 24 px text; result titles use 28 px. Pane headings, search status and the primary search button use 32 px. Privacy and download information forms one paragraph; privacy inferences are described as possibilities using “may reveal”. The plants and sunlight example returns ten results using 32,768 clusters, SQ6 centroids and four probes. The original captured search used an active inference worker and no-store requests for vector and result-text downloads. Displayed timings are separate from the retained paper measurements. No search or measurements were rerun for this capture. Both PNG sizes are rendered at native resolution, not upscaled.
- `screenshots/lab-desktop-4000w.png`: browser capture (4000×2308 pixels), with a separate `screenshots/lab-desktop-2000w.png` copy (2000×1154 pixels), rendered on November 4, 2026 at a 1000-pixel logical width and device scales of 4× and 2× respectively. Enlarged controls and table text show the three four-probe rows (8,192, 16,384 and 32,768 clusters) from the recorded ten-configuration run with noise magnitude 0.4 and 100 queries per configuration. The capture omits the redundant vectors-per-cluster column, masthead and secondary explanatory/status text. No measurements were rerun; displayed timings are illustrative. Both PNG sizes are rendered at native resolution, not upscaled.
- `architecture.pdf`: model, WebAssembly runtime and centroid startup downloads, followed by the five local-search steps and selective static-file downloads. Its DejaVu Sans fonts are embedded using the font files bundled with matplotlib.
- `agreement-bytes.pdf` shows top-1 agreement versus mean gzipped vector payload for 16 FP16 settings at noise magnitude 0.4. The curves show probes 2, 3, 4 and 5 at 8k, and 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 10 at 16k and 32k. Shading shows pointwise 95% Wilson intervals over 10,000 queries per setting.
- `agreement-time.pdf` shows the same settings against observed mean retrieval time, including routing, vector download, decompression, decoding and ranking.
- `large-count-table.tex` and rows: the three four-probe count settings. Historical count/probe measurements remain in `../results/retrieval-summary.csv`.
- `large-compression-table.tex` and rows: all six centroid formats at 32k and four probes.

`probe-large.pdf` shows the 32k FP16 probe sweep with median latency, its interquartile range, and top-1 agreement with 95% Wilson intervals.

`probe-32k-summary.csv` preserves timing quartiles. `../results/top1-agreement.csv` supplies agreement counts and `review-derived.tex` supplies the generated default agreement and confidence interval.
