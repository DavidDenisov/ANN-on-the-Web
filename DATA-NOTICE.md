# Data attribution

The application searches Wikipedia passages and embeddings distributed by [Narek Maloyan](https://huggingface.co/datasets/maloyan/wikipedia-22-12-en-embeddings-all-MiniLM-L6-v2). The source snapshot is identified as December 2022. Embeddings use [all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2); the query client uses its pinned [Xenova conversion](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2).

Wikipedia text belongs to its contributors and remains subject to the applicable [Wikipedia copyright and CC BY-SA terms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights). Result links attribute the source articles, whose histories identify contributors. Passages are extracted and batched from the archived dataset; current articles can differ. Screenshots and the paper's example quote attributed Wikipedia passages.

The Maloyan dataset card was checked on October 30, 2026 and has no separate license declaration. No independent embedding redistribution permission is asserted here; clarify it with the dataset publisher before redistributing embeddings. The source model's license does not by itself establish the dataset's license.

The full corpus and prepared vector/text assets are not included in this GitHub repository. Inter fonts retain their bundled OFL license, and the ACM class retains its original notices and sources. No blanket license for third-party material is implied by publication of this project.
